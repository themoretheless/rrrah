3 | For Digital Service at CMS use only; Not for distribution
  New open source tool for ACOs

   Last year, CMS held listening sessions with ACOs to get feedback on
   challenges with eCQM reporting.

   The OSPO listened to ACOsʼ feedback and identified a way to make eCQM
   reporting easier.

   Then the OSPO built open source software to help ACOs deduplicate
   patient records when reporting eCQMs. This tool is called “DedupliFHIR.”

  User research to improve the tool

   Why are we doing user research?

   • The only guaranteed way to improve a product is to test the product with the
         people who will use it.

   • This means we need to speak with technical ACO staﬀ who prepare eCQMs to
         report to CMS.




5 | For Digital Service at CMS use only; Not for distribution
  Request for research participants

   If an ACO can answer “yes” to the questions below, weʼd like to talk to
   one technical ACO staﬀ member:
✔ Does your ACO have multiple Electronic Health Records systems that pose
         challenges for eCQM reporting?

✔ Does your ACO lack suﬀicient IT support internally?
✔ Do you have an individual on staﬀ who works directly on deduplicating data used
         for reporting eCQMs — someone whoʼs very hands-on? (Not a vendor.)

✔ If you do, has that person done deduplication work for at least 1 cycle of reporting
         eCQMs to CMS?

  Whatʼs involved?

   Weʼre asking for 60-75 minutes of time from 5 to 8 ACO staﬀ members
   (each from a diﬀerent ACO) in the next couple weeks.

   During the research session, we will:
    • Show the staﬀ member our open source tool and ask them to do key tasks
    • Ask questions about the ACOʼs eCQM reporting process, focusing on deduplication
         (workflow and technical questions)

    • Collect all feedback and keep it confidential



7 | For Digital Service at CMS use only; Not for distribution
  How this research helps ACOs

   Each ACO staﬀ memberʼs feedback will help us understand ACO needs.

   Weʼre designing DedupliFHIR for ACOs that:
    • donʼt have much IT support,
    • have staﬀ who work directly on deduplicating data used for reporting eCQMs, and
    • have multiple Electronic Health Records systems.


   By gaining a better understanding of ACO needs, we can improve our open
   source tool and plan future features to fit ACO needs.
